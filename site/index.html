Questions

What does it cost?

Skye is free while in beta. The coding runs on your own Claude plan, so you pay Anthropic as you already do; Skye doesn't resell tokens.

Who can see my code?

Each person gets their own machine. Your repositories, your Claude sign-in and your GitHub sign-in live only on it, and only you can talk to it.

Will it push to my main branch?

No. Every task works on its own skye/… branch, and the pull request is yours to review and merge.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. Skye is open source (Apache 2.0) and runs on your own computer too: github.com/sankhya-ai/Skye.
